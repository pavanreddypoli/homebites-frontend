## 1. Parties

This Chef Agreement ("**Agreement**") is between **RedCube Group LLC**, a Texas limited liability company doing business as **HomeBites AI** (collectively "**HomeBites AI**," "**we**," "**us**"), operating the platform at homebitesai.com, and you, the individual home chef ("**Chef**," "**you**").

By checking the acceptance box during onboarding, you agree to this Agreement.

---

## 2. What HomeBites AI Is (and Isn't)

HomeBites AI operates an online marketplace technology platform (the "**Platform**") that connects independent home chefs with consumers seeking home-cooked meals.

**HomeBites AI does not:**
- Prepare, cook, package, store, transport, or sell any food
- Employ Chefs or direct how Chefs cook
- Inspect, license, or certify Chefs' kitchens
- Act as the merchant of record for food sales

**Chef is solely responsible** for the food Chef prepares, lists, sells, packages, and makes available for pickup.

---

## 3. Independent Contractor Relationship

You are an **independent contractor**, not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of HomeBites AI. Specifically:

- You set your own menu, prices, hours, and capacity
- You decide which orders to accept or decline
- You provide your own kitchen, equipment, ingredients, and supplies
- You determine how you prepare food
- You pay your own taxes; HomeBites AI will issue tax forms only as required by law via Stripe
- Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship

---

## 4. Texas Cottage Food Law Compliance (REQUIRED)

You represent and warrant that you operate as a **Cottage Food Production Operation (CFPO)** under Texas Health & Safety Code Chapter 437 as amended by Senate Bill 541 (effective Sept 1, 2025), and that:

### 4.1 Eligibility (Attestations — must check all)
- [ ] You are an **individual** (not an LLC, corporation, or partnership) producing food in your own home kitchen in Texas
- [ ] Your annual gross sales of cottage foods (across all channels, not just HomeBites AI) will not exceed **$150,000** (the Texas statutory cap, indexed for inflation)
- [ ] You hold a current, DSHS-accredited **food handler certification**, valid for two years, and you will upload a copy before activation
- [ ] If you sell Time/Temperature Control for Safety (TCS) foods, you have registered with Texas DSHS and will provide your registration ID

### 4.2 Prohibited Items
You agree **NOT** to list, sell, or fulfill any item containing:
- Meat or poultry (beef, pork, chicken, turkey, duck, lamb, etc.)
- Seafood, fish, or shellfish
- Ice, ice cream, gelato, sorbet, or popsicles
- Low-acid canned goods
- Raw milk or raw milk products
- CBD, THC, or cannabis-derived ingredients

You acknowledge that listing prohibited items is a **material breach** and grounds for immediate removal from the Platform.

### 4.3 Labeling
You agree to label every food item with:
- Your name and address (or DSHS registration number)
- Product name
- All allergens present (eggs, milk, wheat, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, fish, shellfish)
- The mandatory disclaimer: **"THIS PRODUCT WAS PRODUCED IN A PRIVATE RESIDENCE THAT IS NOT SUBJECT TO GOVERNMENTAL LICENSING OR INSPECTION."**
- For TCS foods: production date and **"SAFE HANDLING INSTRUCTIONS: To prevent illness from bacteria, keep this food refrigerated or frozen until the food is prepared for consumption."**

HomeBites AI will provide a downloadable label PDF generator. Use of it does not relieve Chef of compliance responsibility.

### 4.4 Sales Channel Limits
- All orders are for **in-Texas customers only**
- All orders are **pickup at Chef's home, or personal delivery by Chef, an employee, or a household member** — Chef shall not use third-party couriers (DoorDash, Uber, gig drivers, etc.) under this Agreement
- Chef shall not ship food across state lines

## 5. Listings, Pricing, Payments

### 5.1 Listings
Chef controls all menu listings. Chef warrants that all dishes, photos, descriptions, and ingredient lists are accurate, lawful, and non-infringing.

### 5.2 Pricing
Chef sets prices. Customers pay: subtotal + service fee (5%) + tax (8.25%) = total. Service fee is HomeBites AI's platform fee.

### 5.3 Payment Processing
Chef will onboard as a **Stripe Connect** account. Chef is the merchant of record for every sale. Stripe will deposit Chef's portion of each completed order to Chef's linked bank account on Stripe's standard payout schedule. HomeBites AI never holds Chef funds.

### 5.4 Taxes
Chef is responsible for all income taxes and any sales tax owed on prepared/hot food sales in Texas. HomeBites AI may collect and remit marketplace facilitator sales tax once required by law.
