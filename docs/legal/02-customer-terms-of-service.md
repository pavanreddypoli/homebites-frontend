Orders are **pickup only at the Chef's location**, or, if the Chef offers it, personal delivery by the Chef. Pickup time and location are set by the Chef. Bring ID matching the order name when picking up.

Once a Chef has accepted your order, cancellation may not be possible. Refund requests are evaluated case-by-case. HomeBites AI may issue platform-fee refunds in its discretion; Chef-portion refunds are at the Chef's discretion.
