## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how **RedCube Group LLC**, a Texas limited liability company doing business as **HomeBites AI** ("**HomeBites AI**," "**we**," "**us**"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website, mobile app, and services (the "**Platform**") at homebitesai.com.

---

## 2. What We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account info:** name, email, password (hashed), role (customer / home chef)
- **Customer info:** delivery/pickup address, phone (optional)
- **Chef info:** restaurant name, cuisine, kitchen address, hours, delivery radius, food handler certification, photo IDs, DSHS registration ID (if applicable)
- **Order info:** items, prices, special instructions, order history
- **Payment info:** processed by **Stripe** — we do not store your card number. We store a Stripe customer/account ID and last-4 / brand for display.
- **Communications:** messages you send us via support, email, or in-app

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & log data:** IP address, browser, OS, device identifiers, pages viewed, timestamps
- **Location:** approximate location from IP, or precise location if you grant permission (used to show nearby Chefs)
- **Cookies & similar tech:** session cookies for login, analytics cookies (see Section 8)

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Stripe:** payment status, payout details (for Chefs)
- **Supabase:** authentication metadata
- **Resend:** email delivery status

---

## 3. How We Use It

We use your info to:
- Operate the Platform (accounts, listings, orders, payments)
- Connect customers with Chefs and facilitate transactions
- Send transactional emails (order confirmations, status updates, receipts)
- Provide customer support
- Detect fraud, abuse, and policy violations
- Comply with legal obligations
- Improve the Platform (analytics, AI feature development)
- With your consent, send marketing communications (you can opt out anytime)

---

## 4. How We Share It

We share information only as described below.

### 4.1 With Chefs (when you place an order)
When you order, the Chef sees: your name, contact email, order details, and pickup time. This is necessary for fulfillment.

### 4.2 With Customers (when you're a Chef)
Customers see your restaurant name, listings, neighborhood/city, and approximate location. They do not see your home address until they place an order with you.

### 4.3 With Service Providers
We share data with vendors who help us run the Platform under confidentiality and data-protection obligations:
- **Supabase** (database, auth, storage)
- **Stripe** (payments, payouts, identity verification)
- **Resend** (email delivery)
- **Vercel** (hosting)
- **Anthropic** (AI features, when used)

### 4.4 Legal & Safety
We may disclose info to comply with law, respond to subpoenas, protect our rights, or address fraud or safety issues.

### 4.5 Business Transfers
If HomeBites AI is acquired, merged, or sells assets, your info may transfer — subject to this Policy.

### 4.6 We Do NOT Sell Your Personal Information
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California or Texas privacy law.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We keep info as long as your account is active. After deletion, we may retain limited info (order history, tax records, fraud signals) for up to **7 years** to meet legal/accounting obligations, and aggregated/anonymized data indefinitely.

---

## 6. Your Rights

### 6.1 All Users
You can:
- Access, correct, or delete your account info in settings
- Export your data on request
- Opt out of marketing emails (unsubscribe link in every marketing email)

### 6.2 Texas Residents (TDPSA)
Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, you may:
- Confirm whether we process your personal data
- Access, correct, or delete it
- Obtain a portable copy
- Opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data, or significant profiling

To exercise rights, email [privacy@homebitesai.com]. We will respond within 45 days.

### 6.3 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)
You have similar rights including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale/sharing. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

### 6.4 EU/UK Residents (GDPR/UK-GDPR)
If you access the Platform from the EU/UK, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Our legal bases are: contract (to provide service), legitimate interest (security, analytics), consent (marketing), and legal obligation. Contact [privacy@homebitesai.com].

---

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures: encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest (Supabase), Row-Level Security policies, hashed passwords, and limited internal access. No system is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Cookies

We use:
- **Strictly necessary cookies** (login, session) — always on
- **Analytics cookies** — usage stats, with consent where required

You can manage cookies in your browser settings. EU/UK users will see a consent banner.

---

## 9. Children

The Platform is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided info, email [privacy@homebitesai.com] and we will delete it.
